Hunger Free Kansas Hunger Free Kansas: Cowley County

K-State Extension · 2026 · 2:45

The brief

K-State Extension’s Cowley County project aimed to increase awareness of local food and nutrition resources, including the RISE Cowley Resource Directory. Its review asked for clearer participant identification, more relevant footage, and a closing invitation to contact Extension.

My contribution and the work

I produced and edited a short film connecting interviews with pantry volunteers, food distribution organizers, Extension, and other local partners. The reviewed cut ends with Extension contact information. The available records show the film and requested revisions; they do not establish whether awareness or use of resources increased.
