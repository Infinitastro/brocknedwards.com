The Table We Share

Docushort · Emmaus House, Garden City, Kansas · 6:49

About

In America’s breadbasket, a Kansas town confronts the paradox of hunger amid abundance — revealing how community, care, and courage redefine what it means to share the table.

Shot by shot

  1. 0:52

    Title

    The title lands over a Garden City street, a few seconds after the film’s opening question: “So what right do you have to judge?”

  2. 1:25

    Little free library

    A red little free library beside the stairs at Emmaus House, while the story of how the house “evolved into the needs of the community” is told: longer shelter, food access.

  3. 1:48

    In the doorway

    A man in a work jacket stands in a doorway as the scale comes in: “about 140 to 240 families that come here to seek food.”

  4. 5:12

    Outside Emmaus House

    A woman wrapped in a blanket, backlit by low winter sun, just after “That’s what I want people to know about Emmaus House.”

  5. 5:28

    Unloading

    Hands pass boxes down from a truck. No dialogue; the work carries the scene.

  6. 5:57

    Across the lot

    A woman pushes a loaded cart across an empty lot, a wide frame that holds her small against the town.
