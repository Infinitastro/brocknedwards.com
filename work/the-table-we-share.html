Docushort The Table We Share

Emmaus House, Garden City, Kansas · 6:49

The project

Made with K-State’s Engaged Stories Lab for its Hungry Heartland project, this short documentary follows Emmaus House in Garden City, Kansas, where a student and faculty team filmed interviews with staff and community members about food access and shelter.

My contribution

I co-directed with Jessica Ramirez and Evan Gagny, filmed as part of a three-person video team, and edited the finished documentary. Ramirez coordinated and conducted interviews in English and Spanish. The film was published by K-State and received festival recognition; those are film achievements, not evidence of a measured change in food access.
