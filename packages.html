The fine print How pricing works

  • My rate is $80/hour, billed on actual hours.
  • After a short scoping call you get a flat quote, and I ask for written approval before going past it.
  • I invoice at final delivery on 15-day terms. You can publish the video as soon as it is delivered.
  • Music and stock sound effects through my subscriptions are included in the finished video at no extra charge.
  • Editable project files are not included by default; if you want them, I quote them separately at $80/hour.
