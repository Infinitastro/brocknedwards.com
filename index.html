About

Brock Edwards

I'm a documentary filmmaker and sociologist. Visual ethnography is at the center of how I work: I spend time with people before I point a camera at them, and the films come out of that time.

I hold a master's in sociology from Kansas State University and am a PhD student in sociology at the University of Tennessee, where I also teach. I take on documentary and video work for organizations and educators.
